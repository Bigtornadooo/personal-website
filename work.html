01
Huawei Tech4City Hackathon · Jul 2026 – present

Live-Sync

An AI lecture-comprehension platform that combines speech-to-text, a vision-language model and a vector database, so a student can ask questions of a lecture as it happens. The genuinely interesting problem was cost: I modelled the quality/price tradeoff as a dynamic routing problem between models, which cut inference spend by over 80% without a quality drop we could measure. Pitched the business case to judges on the accessibility track.
